How to read the figures

Each figure is printed as the report prints it, with its unit: 5,537,934 Rs. '000 means Rs. 5,537,934 thousand. Brackets are a loss. They come from the financial statements, the group's column for the report's own year. Open a report for its page numbers.
A report that words a line differently has no figure for it here, but every table is saved whole: see Search the pages.
